# BendlessTech Brand Guidelines

## Brand Identity

**Brand Name:** BendlessTech  
**Tagline:** Technology That Adapts

**Brand Philosophy:**  
We don't make businesses bend to fit technology—we make technology bend to fit businesses.

---

## Logo Usage

### Primary Logo
Use `logo.svg` for light backgrounds.

### Dark Logo
Use `logo-dark.svg` for dark backgrounds.

### Icon Logo
Use `logo-icon.svg` for favicons, app icons, and small spaces.

### Clear Space
Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "B" in "Bendless" on all sides.

### Minimum Size
- Full logo: Minimum width of 150px
- Icon only: Minimum size of 32px

### Don'ts
- Don't distort or stretch the logo
- Don't change the logo colors
- Don't add effects (shadows, gradients, etc.)
- Don't place on busy backgrounds

## Typography

### Primary Font
**Inter** (Google Fonts)
- Fallback: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif

### Font Weights
- Light: 300 (rarely used)
- Regular: 400 (body text)
- Medium: 500 (subheadings)
- Semi-Bold: 600 (emphasis, buttons)
- Bold: 700 (headings)

### Font Sizes

**Desktop:**
- H1: 48px (Hero headings)
- H2: 36px (Section headings)
- H3: 28px (Subsection headings)
- H4: 20px (Card titles)
- Body: 16px (Regular text)
- Small: 14px (Helper text, captions)

**Mobile:**
- H1: 32px
- H2: 28px
- H3: 24px
- H4: 18px
- Body: 16px
- Small: 14px

### Line Height
- Headings: 1.2
- Body text: 1.6-1.8

## Design Style

### Overall Aesthetic
- Modern and sleek
- Professional and trustworthy
- Clean and minimalist
- User-friendly
- Nigerian market-focused

### UI Elements

**Buttons:**
- Primary: Blue background, white text, 6-8px border-radius
- Secondary: Orange background, white text
- Hover: Slightly darker shade + subtle lift effect
- Padding: 12-16px vertical, 24-40px horizontal

**Cards:**
- White background
- Subtle shadow: `0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.1)`
- Border-radius: 8-12px
- Padding: 25-40px

**Forms:**
- Input border: 2px solid #eee
- Focus state: Border changes to primary blue
- Border-radius: 6px
- Padding: 12-16px

**Icons:**
- Use emoji where appropriate for friendliness
- Solid style icons (not outlined)
- Consistent sizing within sections

### Spacing
- Use multiples of 8px for consistency
- Common values: 8px, 16px, 20px, 24px, 30px, 40px, 60px, 80px

## Voice & Tone

### Brand Voice
- Professional yet approachable
- Confident but not arrogant
- Clear and straightforward
- Solution-oriented
- Nigerian-friendly (understands local context)

### Writing Style
- Use active voice
- Be direct and concise
- Avoid jargon unless necessary
- Use Nigerian market examples and context
- Be human and conversational

### Examples

**Good:**
- "Get your quote in 15 minutes via WhatsApp"
- "We build software that adapts to YOUR business"
- "Nigerian businesses lose millions yearly to poor inventory management"

**Avoid:**
- "Utilize our solutions to optimize your paradigm"
- "Best-in-class synergistic platform"
- Complex technical jargon without explanation
